## What evidence is included?

Follow the documentation beside the data: DATA-DICTIONARY.md defines columns, and METHOD.md records dates, observation units and exclusions. Listing captures cover October 22–November 12, 2026 and support 11 checks for 32 comparison entries plus Welnexx; V1 and PR are overlapping views. Other files preserve 134 complaints within 162 language records, topics for 1,184 review IDs, a 23-listing temperature comparison and named approaches reported in a 34-person selection. These source families were counted or captured November 12, 2026; their review/discussion collection intervals are not stated. Keep the differently defined regulatory measures separate.

## What this data does not show

Repository contents are published tables, not a review-ID archive or device-test log. They establish no measured skin temperature, individual limit, isolated product effect, service life or population rate. Reports do not verify injury causation; overlapping discussion categories are not independent outcomes.
